A weekend in Nazare

The place of the 100 foot waves.

Take a relaxing trip to the beach and experience the waves. Nazare is known for the highest waves on earth. Both the men's and women's guiness book of world records for surfing the tallest waves have taken place here. But, during the summer the sun is out and the waves are calm. Enjoy a day at the beach and grab a cool drink from one of the nearby vendors.
