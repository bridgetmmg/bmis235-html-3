4 Nights in Porto

Discover all the city has to offer

On Day 1 explore the city and take a cooking class to learn to make Portugals most famous dessert, Pastel de Nada. Day 2, book a tour to visit the vinards that make port wine and have some tastings. On Day 3 visit the library that inspired Harry Potter, and walk the botanical gardens. There is a high possibility of seeing a peacock. On your last day, cross the river and take a tour of the famous port distilleries, of course tasting what they have to offer.p>
