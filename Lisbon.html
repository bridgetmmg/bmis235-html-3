Five Days in Lisbon

How to split your days to see it all

Day one You are going to go to the church on the hill, spend your day near the old basillica and visit the theives market. Make sure to check out all the amazing vendors and art. Day 2 you are going to head down to the water to visit the tower of Belem and see the parliment building. On Day 3 take the train to Pena Palace and se the outstanding architectural development that the palace it. Spend the rest of your day in this area and taste some tasty food. Day 4, go to the view and experience the amazing entertainment of Fado.Day 5 is your travel day, make the stop to Fatima to experience the spiritual sight.
